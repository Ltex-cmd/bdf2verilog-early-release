# Code of conduct

This applies to issues, pull requests and other project discussions. People of
all backgrounds and experience levels are welcome.

- Be respectful and keep feedback focused on the work
- Explain disagreements with evidence; accept that people make mistakes
- Help others without mocking their skills, language or use of AI tools
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, use sexualized abuse or make personal attacks
- Do not post someone else's private information without permission
- Keep discussions relevant; avoid spam and repeated disruption

## Handling problems

For a problem in a public discussion, alert the repository maintainer
[@Ltex-cmd](https://github.com/Ltex-cmd) in that thread and link the relevant
comment. Do not repeat abusive content or expose private information while
reporting it. If doing so publicly would be unsafe, use GitHub's
[reporting abuse tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
instead. The project does not currently provide a separate private conduct inbox.

The maintainer may ask participants to change their behavior, remove content,
close or lock a discussion, or restrict participation when needed. Decisions
should be proportionate to the behavior and its effect on others. This is a small
volunteer project; no response time is promised.
